## Notes

- THE RECOGNISER IS AN ORACLE (eval/phase3.py OracleStt): it answers from the ground-truth words with seeded drop/mishear/jitter errors. The offline TTS (English flite only) cannot be recognised by a real STT, so these numbers test the audio pipeline (VAD segmentation, timing, PII rules, merging, fail-safe) and the engine's mute application, not recognition accuracy. A native-TTS or recorded-audio run with a real STT is still open.
- Mute coverage is read from OUTPUT audio: spans are applied by engine.audio_mute.MuteBook to the 48 kHz clip as the delay buffer would (delay 4.0 s); a span ready after its samples left is late and leaks.
- The PII LLM (Groq) is absent in every leg (no key, offline): ambiguous spans use the rules' own fallback.
- Mouth blur is checked at the audio->engine hand-off (request windows plus engine.mouth.resolve_mouth_quads, perfect vision on every 3rd frame), not on composited pixels.
- SS_FORCE_API_DOWN is not read by the product code today; the api_down leg injects provider failures itself.
