## Notes

- The classify leg runs with NO LLM (offline, no key): borderline messages are held for a moderator (HOLD_FOR_MOD, counted as flagged), never allowed. The classify_llm leg (informational) uses a stand-in Groq that answers from the truth labels with 5 % errors, so it only shows the cascade's behaviour with an LLM.
- The set is written independently of chat/classify (own vocabulary, spellings, code-mixing, obfuscation and hard negatives). Messages marked not-gating (honestly ambiguous: mild banter, payment lures with no id) are reported by kind but left out of the gating rates.
- Scam recall counts a hit when the verdict carries a scam-link or fake-UPI category; fake-UPI recall needs the fake-UPI category; precision of a group = flagged in that group that truly belong to its label family.
- Connector legs use eval/mock_platforms (own YouTube and Twitch mocks, not chat/testing) with the real connector code and a ManualClock (virtual time), over loopback HTTP and WebSocket.
- Log scan: root logger at DEBUG for the whole leg; every record is searched for flagged message texts, staff texts, the streamer's private terms and phone. Counts only; nothing is printed.
- The RTMPS sink of spec 5.7 is not part of this gate (egress is covered by relay/ tests and gate 1).
